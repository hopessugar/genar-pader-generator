Write the "Trends and Important Observations" section of the PADER.

This section identifies meaningful patterns in the reporting data. Cover:
- Monthly case volume trends (increases, decreases, or stability)
- Geographic distribution patterns
- Any notable reaction concentration patterns

CRITICAL: A statistical or numerical pattern is not automatically a safety signal. Present observations factually and allow a qualified reviewer to determine significance. For example:

GOOD: "Reports increased from X cases in [month] to Y cases in [month]."
BAD: "This increase represents an emerging safety signal."

Only make the second kind of statement if the evidence packet explicitly supports it. Otherwise, present the observation and note that further evaluation may be warranted.
