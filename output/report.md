## 1. Reporting Period {#reporting_period}

This Periodic Adverse Drug Experience Report (PADER) is submitted for Bisoprolol in accordance with regulatory requirements under 21 CFR 314.80. The reporting period covered by this report is from December 27, 2024, through December 26, 2025.

The dataset utilized for this reporting period contained 1,068 total rows, representing 1,024 unique cases. Six flagged rows were excluded from reaction-level analyses due to a reaction/outcome count mismatch. Additionally, System Organ Class (SOC)-level analysis was unavailable for this period as there was no SOC field in the dataset, and reactions were reported at the MedDRA Preferred Term level only. An expectedness assessment was out of scope for this report because no product label or Company Core Data Sheet (CCDS) was supplied.

## 5. Serious Cases / 15-Day Alerts {#serious_cases}

During the reporting period from December 27, 2024, to December 26, 2025, a total of 1,024 unique cases were reported for Bisoprolol. Of these, 1,023 cases (99.9%) were classified as serious, and 1 case (0.1%) was classified as non-serious. 

An analysis of expedited reporting status showed that 1,023 cases (99.9%) met the criteria for expedited 15-day Alerts, while 1 case was non-expedited. The serious and expedited populations within this dataset are nearly identical. This high degree of overlap is expected for spontaneous Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR) data and does not represent an anomaly.

The seriousness criteria for the 1,024 cases were evaluated. Because these criteria are captured as independent flags and are not mutually exclusive, a single case may fulfill multiple seriousness criteria. The breakdown of individual seriousness criteria is as follows:

## Data Limitations

The following limitations apply to this report:

- **SOC-level analysis**: Unavailable. The dataset contains only MedDRA Preferred Terms (PTs), not System Organ Class (SOC) mapping. All reaction analyses are reported at the PT level.
- **Expectedness**: Out of scope. No product label/CCDS was supplied, so labelled/unlabelled classification could not be performed.
- **History of actions**: No action data was supplied with this dataset.
- **Cumulative counts**: Not available. All counts represent the current reporting interval only (no prior-period data).
- **Flagged rows**: Approximately 6 of 1,068 rows (0.6%) were excluded from reaction-level analyses due to reaction/outcome count mismatches caused by MedDRA PTs containing embedded commas (e.g., "Hallucination, visual"). These rows are fully included in all case-level analyses.
